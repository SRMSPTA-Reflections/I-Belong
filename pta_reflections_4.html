About the Reflections Program

Reflections is a long-standing arts recognition initiative managed by National PTA, offering students of all grade levels an inclusive way to express themselves creatively.

  • Founded in 1969, Reflections has positively impacted millions, with over 200,000 students in pre-k through 12 grade participating annually nationwide to create original work of art in response to student created theme.
  • The program emphasizes artistic expression over competition, offering students of all abilities a safe space to explore creative storytelling and build confidence.
  • Reflections builds artistic literacy, emotional expression, and confidence, while promoting family and community engagement.
  • Open to all Texas students enrolled in a school with an active PTA/PTSA—or through the Lone Star Statewide PTA.
  • Each student’s artwork must be an original creation that uniquely interprets the annual theme.
  • The student or at least one person from their primary household must be a current member of the Local PTA/PTSA where the student is participating.

General Overview

  • The PTA Reflections program encourages students to showcase their talents by offering six Arts categories – dance choreography, film production, literature, music composition, photography and 2D visual arts.
  • Participants may enter in one or more categories with no limit to the number of entries in a single category.
  • Each submission is evaluated on how well the student uses artistic vision to portray the theme.
  • Student winners from local level will have the possibility to move forward to council and State, with opportunity to be recognized nationally.
  • Top winners at the national level are offered cash prizes and scholarships.
  • Each work must be accompanied by an Entry form.
  • Artist Statement requirement: All entries must include a title and an artist statement of 10–100 words explaining what inspired the work and how it relates to the theme.

Submission deadline is October 30th.
